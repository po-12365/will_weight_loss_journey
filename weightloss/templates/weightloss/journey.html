The Beginning

In January 2019, I started my journey at 120 kg. At the beginning, I was scared to step into the gym. I felt anxious about lifting weights and worried about what other people might think of me. But over time, I realised something important — most people are simply focused on their own journey. They pass by, and they do not judge me the way I imagined. Looking back, I am proud that I showed up every day, tracked my calories, and stayed consistent even when it felt uncomfortable. This journey was never only about losing weight. It was about building confidence, discipline, and believing that I was capable of changing my own life.

THE FIRST STEP

Starting Small, Staying Consistent

In the early days, my routine was simple. I started with just 10 minutes of jogging on the treadmill at around 9–10 km/h, followed by machine weights.

I did not start with complicated training. There were no squats, deadlifts, or bench presses. I focused on building the habit first and becoming comfortable in the gym environment.

From January 2019 to September 2019, I kept showing up, tracking my calories, and improving step by step. The biggest change was not only physical. I learned that consistency matters more than having the perfect plan.
